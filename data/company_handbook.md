## 3. Office Policies

### Work Timings
- Standard work hours: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Monday to Friday)
- Lunch break: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM
- Flexible timing allowed with manager approval (core hours: 11 AM to 5 PM)
- Saturday and Sunday are off

### Attendance
- All employees must mark attendance daily through the internal portal
- Work-from-home is allowed up to 2 days per week with manager approval
- If working remotely, you must be reachable on Slack during core hours

### Leave Policy
- **Casual Leave:** 12 days per year (can take up to 3 consecutive days)
- **Sick Leave:** 10 days per year (medical certificate required for 3+ days)
- **Earned Leave:** 15 days per year (must apply 7 days in advance)
- **Maternity Leave:** 26 weeks (as per Indian law)
- **Paternity Leave:** 2 weeks
- **Compensatory Off:** If you work on a weekend or holiday, you get a comp-off within 30 days
- Unused earned leave can be carried forward (max 30 days)
- Unused casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward

### Dress Code
- Business casual on weekdays
- Casual on Fridays (jeans and t-shirts are fine)
- No dress code when working from home

## 4. IT Policies

### Asset Assignment
- Every employee is assigned a laptop on their first day
- Monitors, tablets, and peripherals are assigned based on role and department
- Designers get Wacom tablets; developers get dual monitors on request
- All assigned assets are tracked in the IT Asset Management System (SanAsset)

### How to Request a New Asset
1. Raise a request with your manager
2. Manager approves and forwards to Site IT
3. Site IT checks available inventory
4. If available, asset is assigned and updated in the system
5. If not available, Site IT raises a purchase request with Admin

### Lost or Damaged Assets
- Report immediately to Site IT (within 24 hours)
- Site IT will assess the damage and decide: repair or replace
- If the damage is due to negligence, the employee may bear partial cost
- Stolen assets must be reported to both Site IT and Admin

### Software Installation
- Only approved software may be installed on company machines
- To request new software, submit a request to Site IT with justification
- No pirated or unlicensed software is allowed — this is a zero-tolerance policy
- Game engines (Unity, Unreal) are pre-installed on developer and designer machines

### Password Policy
- Minimum 10 characters with at least 1 uppercase, 1 number, and 1 special character
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days
- Do not share your password with anyone, including your manager
- Use the company password manager for all work-related accounts
- Enable two-factor authentication on all company accounts

### VPN and Remote Access
- Use the company VPN when working remotely
- VPN credentials are provided by Site IT on your first day
- Do not access company systems from public WiFi without VPN
- Report any suspicious activity on your account immediately

## 5. Communication Guidelines

### Tools We Use
- **Slack:** Day-to-day communication, team channels, quick questions
- **Email:** Formal communication, external communication, approvals
- **Jira:** Task tracking, bug reports, sprint planning
- **Confluence:** Documentation, meeting notes, knowledge base
- **GitHub:** Code repositories, pull requests, code reviews
- **Google Meet:** Video calls, team standups, client meetings

### Communication Rules
- Respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during work hours
- Use threads in Slack — don't flood the main channel
- Mark yourself as "Away" on Slack if you're not available
- For urgent issues, call the person directly — don't wait for a Slack reply
- All meetings must have an agenda shared at least 1 hour before
- Keep meetings under 30 minutes unless it's a sprint planning or review

### Escalation Path
1. **Level 1:** Talk to your direct manager
2. **Level 2:** Manager escalates to department head
3. **Level 3:** Department head escalates to CEO (Sankhyayana)
- For IT issues: Contact Site IT help desk on Slack (#it-helpdesk)
- For HR issues: Contact Admin department directly

## 8. Code of Conduct

### General Conduct
- Treat everyone with respect — zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- Be punctual for meetings and deadlines

### Confidentiality
- Game designs, source code, and business plans are confidential

### Social Media
- Do not post unreleased game content or screenshots
- You may share official company announcements on your personal accounts
- Add a disclaimer: "Views are my own" when posting opinions
